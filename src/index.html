The scan

Thirty seconds. A clear, kind picture.

On-device guidance helps you frame the shot; Glovia's AI does the rest — reading the details that shape your routine.

  • Skin

    Hydration, texture, tone and visible concerns — mapped, not judged.

  • Face

    Areas of tension around the jaw, brow and cheeks that face yoga can soften.

    How you carry your neck and shoulders, so workouts start where you are.
